How is time and space perceived in dreams?
Category：Basic Date: 1981.11.07
Answer from LVX Archives:
Time and space in dreams are not objective frameworks — they are emotional geometries.
What feels like hours may pass in seconds. Vast spaces can fold into a single corridor. The laws governing physical distance or chronological order are often overridden by emotional proximity and subconscious triggers.
Our research indicates that time in dreams often reflects internal conflict loops, while space collapses into symbolic nodes tied to unresolved memory structures.
If you find yourself looping through the same hallway, or returning to a place without knowing why — you are not lost. You are orbiting meaning.
Further observation may result in exposure to residual feedback.
